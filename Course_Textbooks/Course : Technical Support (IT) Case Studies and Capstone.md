The **Technical Support (IT) Case Studies and Capstone** is the final course (Course 7 of 7) in the **IBM** **IT Support Professional Certificate** offered on **Coursera** and **edX**. This intermediate-level capstone course is designed to apply the foundational IT knowledge gained from the previous six courses to realistic, hands-on professional scenarios.

Key features of the course include:

*   **Hands-On Case Studies**: Learners resolve diverse technical issues across five core domains:
    *   **Hardware and Operating Systems**: Troubleshooting hardware failures and OS fundamentals.
    *   **Software, Programming, and Databases**: Managing software updates, browser issues, and database permissions.
    *   **Networking and Storage**: Diagnosing network connectivity errors and configuring storage solutions.
    *   **Cybersecurity**: Mitigating cyber threats and securing user information.
    *   **Cloud Computing**: Managing cloud access policies, IAM, and cloud services.
*   **Professional Skills**: The course emphasizes systematic troubleshooting, the use of industry-standard ticketing systems, remote diagnostic tools, and customer service skills such as conflict mitigation and de-escalation.
*   **Certification Preparation**: It includes a final exam that simulates the **CompTIA IT Fundamentals (ITF+)** Certification Exam. Successful completion of the entire IBM IT Support Professional Certificate provides a 30% discount voucher for the CompTIA ITF+ exam.
*   **Duration and Format**: The course typically requires approximately 10 hours of study time and is available in multiple languages.

For the **Technical Support (IT) Case Studies and Capstone** course and the associated **CompTIA IT Fundamentals (ITF+)** exam preparation, the following textbooks are widely considered the most effective resources:

### **Top Recommended Textbooks**

**1. CompTIA IT Fundamentals (ITF+) Study Guide: Exam FC0-U61** by *Quentin Docter*
This is arguably the **best comprehensive textbook** specifically for the ITF+ certification objectives covered in the capstone. It covers 100% of the exam objectives, including IT concepts, infrastructure, applications, software development, database fundamentals, and security. It includes practical examples, review questions, and access to online study tools like flashcards and practice exams.

**2. ITF+ CompTIA IT Fundamentals All-in-One Exam Guide** by *Mike Meyers, Scott Jernigan, and Daniel Lachance*
Known for its **accessible writing style** and deep technical insight, this guide is excellent for beginners. It breaks down complex topics like hardware, OS, security, and troubleshooting into easy-to-understand sections, making it highly suitable for the hands-on case studies in the IBM capstone.

**3. A+ Guide to IT Technical Support (Hardware and Software)** by *Jean Andrews*
While technically aimed at the more advanced **CompTIA A+** certification, this book is frequently recommended for IT support learners because of its **exceptional troubleshooting methodology**. It provides a step-by-step, highly visual approach to resolving hardware and software issues, which directly mirrors the real-world scenarios presented in the IBM capstone projects.

### **Supplementary Free Resource**
*   **Community-Generated Notes**: Highly detailed, textbook-quality notes covering the entire IT Support curriculum (Networking, OS, SysAdmin, Security) are available for free via community contributions (often found in PDF or Google Doc formats shared by past learners on platforms like Reddit). These can serve as a concise alternative to a physical textbook for quick revision.

For the specific **Capstone** portion, the primary "textbook" is effectively the **course material itself**, as it relies on applying knowledge to simulated tickets and case studies rather than theoretical reading. However, using **Docter's Study Guide** alongside the labs will provide the strongest theoretical foundation for the final exam.

### **3. Cybersecurity & Information Assurance**
*   **Title**: ***Security in Computing***
